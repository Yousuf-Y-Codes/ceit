Computer Engineering Item Tracker

Lab Library

A fast, no-clutter web tool for Mr. Kidston's Computer Engineering lab. Sign out microcontrollers, sensors, cables, and meters by scanning a bin QR code or entering its short code.

  • 1. Single-Screen Check-Out Scan a bin QR label or type the component code, select your class period (Period 1–4), and confirm with default due dates.
  • 2. Live Student Dashboard View every component currently checked out under your student number, check due countdowns, and return items when done.
  • 3. Teacher Admin Panel Filter active loans by Period 1–4 or overdue status, manage stock, print cut-out QR bin sheets, and export audit CSVs.
PDSB Access Only (@pdsb.net): Student emails (<digits>@pdsb.net) automatically set your student number. Teacher emails (p<digits>@pdsb.net) automatically open the Teacher Panel.
