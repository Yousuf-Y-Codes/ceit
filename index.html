Welcome to the Computer Engineering Item Tracker

Mr. Kidston's Lab

CEIT is a digital sign-out library for the Peel District School Board Computer Engineering lab. Sign in with your school account to borrow microcontrollers, sensors, cables, and tools — and track due dates in real time.

  • 1. Scan a QR Code or Type an Item Code Use your phone camera, lab webcam, or type the printed bin code (e.g. ARD-UNO-01) to check out hardware in seconds.
  • 2. Track Your Borrowed Components & Due Dates View every item currently signed out to your student number, check due dates, and return parts back to inventory when your lab is complete.
  • 3. Built-In Teacher Admin Panel Teachers can monitor all active sign-outs across the class, add new hardware components, and print cut-out QR code bin labels directly from the browser.
PDSB Account Tip: Click Sign In with School Google Account to log in with your @pdsb.net address. If Google OAuth is restricted on your school network, use the Email & Password login or sign-up form on the right.
