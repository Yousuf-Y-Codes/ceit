Sign Out Component for a Student (Guest Mode)

How Guest Mode Works

  • No Student Login Needed If a student forgot their phone/laptop or cannot access Google Sign-In during class, you can immediately record their sign-out under their name and student number.
  • Atomic Stock Tracking Guest sign-outs decrement inventory stock identically to student self-checkouts and appear in Active Sign-Outs with a GUEST badge.
  • Automatic PDSB Email Link Entering a student's numeric ID automatically links <number>@pdsb.net so you can send email reminders with one click.
