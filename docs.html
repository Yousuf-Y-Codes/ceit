1. Project Overview

PDSB Computer Engineering

The Computer Engineering Item Tracker (CEIT) is a lightweight web application built for Mr. Kidston's Computer Engineering lab. It works like a digital library for hardware components (Arduinos, sensors, breadboards, cables, meters, and tools).

  • Students sign in with their school account, scan a component's QR code or enter its text code, select a loan duration, and monitor due dates from their dashboard.
  • Teachers view every signed-out component in real time, add/edit lab inventory, print cut-out QR code bin labels, record guest sign-outs, and export audit logs to CSV.
  • Zero App Installs Required: Runs in any modern desktop or mobile web browser using pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Firebase.

2. Signing In & Role Routing (index.html)

Option A — School Google Sign-In (Recommended)

  1. Open the CEIT portal (ceit.site).
  2. Click Sign In with School Google Account.
  3. Select your Peel District School Board Google account (only @pdsb.net emails are allowed).
  4. Your student number (<digits>@pdsb.net) or teacher status (p<digits>@pdsb.net) is detected automatically from your email address.

Option B — Email & Password Fallback

If third-party Google OAuth is restricted by school network policy on a lab computer, students and teachers can use the built-in Email & Password login with their @pdsb.net email:

  1. Click Create Account beneath the Sign In button.
  2. Enter your Full Name, your @pdsb.net School Email, and a password (at least 6 characters).
  3. Click Create Account to register and sign in immediately.

Automatic Role Routing & Domain Restriction

Accounts outside @pdsb.net are automatically blocked. Any PDSB teacher email matching p<digits>@pdsb.net (such as Mr. Kidston's p0161177@pdsb.net) is automatically assigned the Teacher role and routed to teacher.html. Numeric student emails (<digits>@pdsb.net) receive the Student role with their student number derived directly from their email and are routed to dashboard.html. If you arrived by scanning a QR code link, CEIT remembers the component and sends you straight to the Check Out page after login.

3. Student Dashboard (dashboard.html)

Student Guide

The Student Dashboard is your home page for tracking borrowed hardware.

Summary Stats Bar

At the top of the page, four live counters display your Checked Out Now total, items Due Within 24h, any Overdue Items, and your Total Returned count.

My Checked Out Items Tab

  • Lists every component currently signed out under your account, including its Item Code, Checked Out timestamp, Due Date, and live status badge (ACTIVE, DUE SOON, or OVERDUE).
  • When you finish using a component and place it back in its lab bin, click Return Item and confirm in the dialog. Inventory stock updates immediately.

My Return History & Available Lab Inventory Tabs

  • My Return History: Shows a timestamped record of all items you have previously returned.
  • Available Lab Inventory: Lets you search the lab's stock by name, code, bin location, or category and click Check Out directly on any in-stock item.

Automatic Student Number Detection

Your student number is automatically pulled from your PDSB email (<digits>@pdsb.net) and attached to every component you sign out — no manual entry needed.

4. Check Out Page (checkout.html)

Student Guide

Click + Check Out New Component from your dashboard (or scan a printed QR label with your phone) to open the Check Out page.

Method 1 — Type a Component Code

  1. Stay on the Type Component Code tab.
  2. Type the bin code (for example, MCU-ARDUN-01) and click Find (or press Enter).
  3. Alternatively, choose any in-stock component from the Quick Select dropdown.

Method 2 — Scan a QR Code

  1. Click the Scan QR Code tab.
  2. Click Start Camera and allow browser camera permission.
  3. Hold the component's QR label inside the green framing square. CEIT automatically detects the code and loads the component details.
  4. No webcam? Use Scan QR Code from Saved Image to upload a photo of the QR label instead.

Confirming Your Sign-Out

  1. Verify the component name, bin location, and available stock on the right-hand card.
  2. Confirm your Student Number.
  3. Choose your Loan Duration (1 Day, 3 Days, 1 Week, 2 Weeks, or Custom 1–30 Days) and inspect the calculated due date.
  4. Optionally add a short note (e.g., lab name) and click Confirm Sign Out. Stock is atomically decremented and you are returned to your dashboard.

5. Teacher Admin Panel — Active Sign-Outs (teacher.html)

Teacher Guide

Only accounts with the teacher role can access teacher.html. Students attempting to open it are automatically redirected to their Student Dashboard.

Monitoring Checked-Out Items

  • The Active Sign-Outs tab displays every item currently out across the entire lab in real time, showing the component name/code, student name/email, Student Number, checkout time, due date, and status badge.
  • Use the search box to look up a specific student number, name, or component code.
  • Use the dropdown filter to isolate Overdue Only, Due Within 24h, or Teacher Guest Sign-Outs.
  • Click Check In to mark a component returned and restore its stock count.
  • Click Email to open a pre-addressed return reminder email to the student's @pdsb.net address.

6. Creating New Items & Printable QR Codes

Teacher Guide

Adding a Component

  1. Open the Inventory & QR Labels tab in teacher.html.
  2. Enter the Component Name (e.g., Arduino Uno R3) and select a Category.
  3. Enter a unique Item Code, or click Auto to generate a clean code automatically (e.g., MCU-ARDUN-01).
  4. Set the Total Quantity, optional Bin / Location, and Notes, then click + Add Component to Inventory.

Editing or Deleting Items

  • Click Edit next to any item to change its name, category, total quantity, bin location, or notes. CEIT prevents lowering total quantity below the number of units currently signed out.
  • Click Delete to remove an obsolete component from inventory (with a confirmation warning if units are currently signed out).

Generating & Printing QR Code Labels

  • Click QR Code on any inventory row to preview its scannable QR label. Each QR encodes a direct deep link (checkout.html?code=...) so students can scan it with either their phone's built-in camera or the CEIT Check Out scanner.
  • From the QR modal, you can Copy Code, Download PNG, or Print Label.
  • Click Print All QR Labels (top right) or Print Visible QR Labels to generate a clean, 3-column cut-out sheet formatted specifically for classroom printing and taping onto lab bins.

7. Guest Sign-Out Mode & Audit History

Teacher Guide

Guest Sign-Out Tab

If a student does not have a device or cannot log in during class, open the Guest Sign-Out tab, enter the student's Full Name and Student Number, choose the component and duration, and click Record Guest Sign-Out. Inventory decrements immediately and the loan appears in Active Sign-Outs with a GUEST badge.

Audit History & CSV Export

The Audit History tab retains a complete chronological log of both active and returned checkouts. Use the search bar or status dropdown to filter logs, or click Download CSV / Export Audit CSV to save a spreadsheet copy for lab records.

8. Troubleshooting & FAQ

  • Google Sign-In popup is blocked or restricted on a PDSB device: Allow popups in the address bar, or switch to the Email & Password login/sign-up form on index.html.
  • Camera won't start on the Check Out page: Ensure the site is loaded over HTTPS (such as https://ceit.site), grant camera permissions when prompted, or use the Type Component Code / Scan QR from Saved Image options.
  • Adding another teacher account: Add the teacher's email address to INITIAL_TEACHER_EMAILS inside js/firebase-config.js, or update their document's role field to "teacher" in Cloud Firestore.
  • Testing live database connectivity: Open the built-in Backend Diagnostic Console to verify Firebase configuration, authentication, and real-time Firestore transactions.
