1. Project Overview

PDSB Computer Engineering

The Computer Engineering Item Tracker (CEIT) is a lightweight web app built for Mr. Kidston’s Computer Engineering lab. It tracks Arduinos, sensors, cables, and tools using pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Firebase with zero external frameworks.

  • Students sign in with their verified @pdsb.net Google account, scan a QR label or type a code, choose their class period (Period 1 to 4), and track due dates.
  • Teachers filter active loans by Period 1 to 4 or overdue status, manage stock, print QR bin label sheets, record guest loans, and export audit CSVs.

2. Signing In & Role Routing (index.html)

Google OAuth Only (@pdsb.net)

Click Sign In with Google and select your verified Peel District School Board (@pdsb.net) Google account. Password-based sign-in is disabled to prevent unverified email impersonation. If a student cannot sign in on a lab computer, teachers can record the loan using Guest Sign-Out in the Teacher Panel.

Automatic Role & Student Number Detection

  • Students (<digits>@pdsb.net): Automatically assigned the student role with their student number extracted from the verified Google email and routed to dashboard.html.
  • Teachers (p<digits>@pdsb.net): Automatically assigned the teacher role (e.g. p0161177@pdsb.net) and routed to teacher.html.
  • Non-@pdsb.net accounts and non-Google sessions are strictly blocked in both the app and Cloud Firestore security rules.

3. Student Dashboard (dashboard.html)

Student guide

Active Loans, Returning Items & Catalog

  • Code Lookup Bar: Type any printed component code (e.g. MCU-ARDUN-01) and click Find to jump straight into the pre-filled Check Out form.
  • Active Loans Tab: Shows every component currently signed out under your student number, its class period, checkout timestamp, due date countdown, and a Return button. After clicking Confirm Return, place the physical component back into its labeled storage bin.
  • Return History Tab: Timestamped log of all components you have previously returned.
  • Lab Catalog Tab: Searchable view of all lab stock, bin locations, availability bars, and direct Check Out links.

4. Single-Screen Check Out Form (checkout.html)

Student guide
  1. Step 1, Select Component: Type the bin code and click Find, pick from the in-stock dropdown, or click Scan QR to open the inline camera scanner (or upload a QR image if your PC has no webcam).
  2. Step 2, Class Period (Required): Explicitly pick Period 1, Period 2, Period 3, or Period 4. No period is pre-selected by default so loans stay accurately grouped.
  3. Step 3, Loan Details & Confirm: Your school account ID is auto-filled and loan duration defaults to 1 Week (7 Days) (adjustable to 1 Day, 3 Days, 2 Weeks, or Custom 1 to 30 days). Click Confirm Sign Out to decrement stock and return to your dashboard.

5. Teacher Panel: Active Loans & Period Filtering (teacher.html)

Teacher guide
  • Period Filter: Use the Period dropdown (All Periods, Period 1 to Period 4) to view only the students in your current class.
  • Overdue Attention Banner & Filter Chips: Immediately spot overdue loans and filter by Overdue, Due Soon, or Guest.
  • Check In & Email Reminders: Return components back into stock with one click or open a pre-filled mailto: reminder to the student’s @pdsb.net address.

6. Inventory & Printable QR Labels

Teacher guide
  • Add components with a name, category, quantity, bin location, and unique item code (or click Auto to generate one).
  • Click QR Label on any row to preview, copy code, download PNG, or print a single label.
  • Click Print All QR Labels or Print Visible QR to print a clean 3-column cut-out sheet for lab bins.
  • Use Edit or Delete to maintain inventory (CEIT prevents reducing total stock below the currently signed-out quantity).

7. Guest Mode & Audit History

Teacher guide

Guest Sign-Out: If a student forgot their device or cannot sign in during class, record a loan under the Guest Sign-Out tab using their name, student number, class period (Period 1 to 4), component, and duration. Entering a student’s numeric ID automatically links <number>@pdsb.net so you can still send one-click email reminders, and the loan appears in Active Sign-Outs with a Guest badge.

Audit History & CSV Export: Filter complete sign-out and return logs by Period, status, or search keyword, and click Download CSV or Export CSV (includes the Period column).

8. Security & Troubleshooting

  • Shared Lab Computer Safety: Sessions automatically expire when the browser tab is closed or after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Email rejected: Only verified @pdsb.net Google accounts are permitted.
  • Camera won’t start: Open CEIT over HTTPS (https://ceit.site), grant browser camera permission, or type the code or upload a QR photo instead.
  • Teacher backend diagnostics: Teachers can visit Backend Diagnostics to test live Firebase auth and Firestore operations.
