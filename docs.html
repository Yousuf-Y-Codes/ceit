1. Project Overview

PDSB Computer Engineering

The Computer Engineering Item Tracker (CEIT) is a digital sign-out library built for Mr. Kidston's Computer Engineering lab. It tracks Arduinos, sensors, cables, and tools without requiring any app downloads or custom scanner hardware.

  • Students sign in with their @pdsb.net email, scan a bin QR code or enter an item code, pick a loan duration, and track return countdowns.
  • Teachers monitor all active loans and overdue alerts in real time, create/edit components, print cut-out QR label sheets, record guest loans, and export CSV audit logs.

2. Signing In & Role Routing (index.html)

Option A — School Google Sign-In

  1. Open ceit.site and click Continue with School Google Account.
  2. Choose your Peel District School Board account (only @pdsb.net emails are permitted).

Option B — Email & Password Fallback

If Google OAuth is restricted on a school computer, click Create a password login and register using your @pdsb.net email address and a password (at least 6 characters).

Automatic Student Number & Teacher Detection

  • Students (<digits>@pdsb.net): Automatically assigned the student role with their student number extracted from the email, routed to dashboard.html.
  • Teachers (p<digits>@pdsb.net): Automatically assigned the teacher role (e.g. p0161177@pdsb.net) and routed to teacher.html.
  • QR Deep Links: Scanning a printed QR label while logged out automatically returns you to that exact component after signing in.

3. Student Dashboard (dashboard.html)

Student Guide

Quick Code Jump & Stats

Type any component code into the top quick-lookup bar to jump straight to check-out, or click Scan QR or Check Out. Four summary cards display your active loans, items due within 24 hours, overdue items, and total returned components.

Tabs

  • Active Loans: Lists every component you currently have out with live countdowns (e.g. Due in 5 days) and a one-click Return button.
  • Return History: Timestamped log of components you have returned.
  • Lab Catalog: Searchable view of lab stock with availability meters and direct Check Out buttons.

4. Check Out Page (checkout.html)

Student Guide

Step 1 — Locate Component

  • Type Code: Enter the printed bin code (e.g. MCU-ARDUN-01) and press Enter, or pick from the in-stock dropdown.
  • Scan QR: Switch to the Scan QR tab, tap Start Camera, and center the bin QR label inside the green box. On desktops without a camera, use Scan QR Code from Image File.

Step 2 — Confirm Sign-Out

  1. Verify the component name, bin location, and available stock.
  2. Tap a loan duration chip (1 Day, 3 Days, 1 Week, 2 Weeks, or Custom 1–30 days) and review the calculated due date.
  3. Optionally add a lab note and click Confirm Sign Out. Stock updates atomically and returns you to your dashboard.

5. Teacher Panel — Active Sign-Outs (teacher.html)

Teacher Guide

Protected by a strict teacher role guard (students are redirected back to dashboard.html).

  • Overdue Attention Banner: Automatically appears whenever any student loan is past due, with a one-click Show Overdue filter button.
  • Search & Filter Chips: Instantly search by student number, name, or item code, or tap All, Overdue, Due Soon, or Guest Mode.
  • Actions: Click Check In to return an item to stock, or Email to open a pre-filled return reminder to the student's @pdsb.net address.

6. Creating Items & Printable QR Labels

Teacher Guide

Adding & Editing Components

  1. Open the Inventory & QR Labels tab.
  2. Enter the component name, category, quantity, and bin location. Click Auto next to Item Code to generate a unique code automatically.
  3. Use Edit to adjust quantities or details (CEIT prevents lowering total quantity below the number currently checked out) or Delete to remove obsolete stock.

Printing QR Bin Labels

  • Click QR Label on any row to preview its scannable QR code, copy the code, download a PNG, or print a single label.
  • Click Print All QR Labels or Print Visible QR to open a clean 3-column cut-out sheet formatted for classroom printing and taping onto storage bins.

7. Guest Mode & Audit History

Teacher Guide

Guest Sign-Out

Use the Guest Sign-Out tab to record a loan for a student who forgot their device or cannot sign in during class. Enter their name, student number, component, and duration — their @pdsb.net email is linked automatically.

Audit History & CSV Export

The Audit History tab preserves all active and returned records. Click Export CSV at any time to download a spreadsheet for grading or end-of-term inventory checks.

8. Troubleshooting

  • Non-PDSB email rejected: Only @pdsb.net school addresses are accepted for security.
  • Camera won't start: Ensure the site is opened via https://ceit.site and camera access is allowed, or use the Type Code / QR Image Upload options.
  • Diagnostics: Visit the Backend Diagnostic Console to test live Firebase Auth and Firestore transactions.
